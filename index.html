WHY WE EXIST

A Space Created for the Consecrated Ones

Spirit Conservation is a traveling retreat created to protect and strengthen a special group of people known as the Consecrated Ones.

These are individuals chosen by the Flow, the Holy Spirit, before time began, who live with deep loyalty to Her.

In today's world, where this way of life is often rejected, these ones are becoming rare. Spirit Conservation exists to support, renew, and preserve them.

THE SOURCE

I AM

The Holy Spirit. Where we came from. The Mother of our being. Our Source God.

A retreat is a time to step away from the noise and intentionally make room for fellowship, prayer, stillness, and renewal.
